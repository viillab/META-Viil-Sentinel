# VIIL-Sentinel — Offline Temporal Multi-Pose Association Audit
27 October 2026 · Member 1 · **research-only, no production selection**

## Reason for this research

The previous real ASSIST-IoT frozen 11-video spatial benchmark exposed
860 MediaPipe two-pose frames among three publisher-labeled Fall videos.
820 showed spatially separated torso geometry and 40 had unusable
torso geometry. The STRICT edge adapter correctly abstains on all
multi-pose frames; selecting the first/strongest pose without evaluating
identity is unsafe and cannot be justified by those exposed clips.
One fully visible fainting Fall remains undetected even without pose
dropout, so association alone cannot fix the event classifier.

We now **measure potential short-term temporal continuity**, NOT
attempt identity recovery or auto-selection. The model remains frozen,
all existing Fall event behavior stays byte-for-byte generated by
the original adapter, and the outputs contain only aggregate categorical
counters.

## Precise bounded algorithm

A separate opt-in `OfflineAssociationObserver` keeps, **in volatile
memory for a single video**, only four torso joint coordinates of
the most recently *strictly accepted single-pose* frame, the torso
centroid/size and its frame index. This is **not a person ID**.

- An anchor is created **only on a strict single-person PoseFrame**,
  after the existing 0.55 visibility/presence and in-frame bounds,
  plus minimum 0.04 normalized torso geometry.
- The temporal horizon is **6 decoded frames**, never 6 seconds.
  The anchor ages even when no pose or multiple poses are returned.
- A frame with two returned poses gets a tentative
  `multiple_short_anchor_unique_candidate` count only if
  both poses have usable torsos, *exactly one* torso centroid lies
  within max(0.04, 0.25 × anchor torso length) of the last valid
  strict single-pose anchor, **AND** the other lies at least
  max(0.22, 0.70 × anchor torso length) from that anchor.
- If both are close, produce `multiple_short_anchor_ambiguous`.
  If neither is close, `multiple_short_anchor_no_match`.
  If anchor was never established or expired, count
  `multiple_anchor_stale_or_missing`.
  Unusable torsos and >2 returned poses have separate abstain classes.
- **A multi-pose frame NEVER refreshes the anchor**, even if it has a
  unique-looking candidate. Only a subsequent STRICT single-person
  frame may re-establish the anchor. All ambiguous/invalid cases
  fail closed, and even the unique-looking cases **remain abstentions
  for the existing fall event detector**.

These hardcoded heuristics were set before evaluating this version
against the 11 previously viewed reference videos; they are not tuned
to those outputs. "Unique candidate" means **only a geometrically
unique temporal hypothesis**, NOT verified same-person association,
individual identity, safe incident detection, face matching or
proof that the duplicate-person issue has been solved.

The original production `extract_torso_landmarks` and fall model
are unchanged. `runner.process_capture` constructs the observer
**only when an opt-in callback is supplied** by the offline
`association-audit` benchmark. It never forwards coordinates,
full frames, person IDs, per-frame timestamps or original confidence
floats. It sends exactly one categorical status per decoded frame.
The benchmark checks that all statuses sum to decoded frames, all
`multiple_*` categories sum to previous exact multi-pose rejections,
and accepted-single categories reconcile with original strict adapter.


use a new filename for a repeat. Only publisher activity names and
categorical integer counts are persisted. No per-frame trajectories,
video or model weight blobs are committed or uploaded.

## Reviewer cautions / next actions

This bounded observer is a **diagnostic baseline**, not a trained
association network. The test videos are already seen. Regardless of
the number of short-horizon candidates, never select/merge a pose in
live Fall alerts or claim better fall recall. To establish genuine
multi-person association, obtain consented new source video with
per-person human-reviewed pose identity and occlusion labels. Evaluate
identity switches, abstention at overlapping people, unseen cameras
and latency on a fresh person-disjoint set. Keep production
multi-person abstention until safety and reliability are proven.
Separately develop onset-annotated temporal Fall recognition for
fully visible missed fainting events.

The next Member1 developer should only merge verified source/tests
into `member/feature/preprod`, not the human-controlled `preprod`
or `main`.

## Actual completed real-video benchmark, 27 October 2026

**Ran successfully** against all 11 original Git-blob-hash-pinned
ASSIST-IoT reference MP4s (8 ADL, 3 Fall) using MediaPipe Lite.
The original strict adapter stayed unchanged and the frozen 30D
research V1 file SHA-256 and threshold **0.53** were verified.
No new incident candidate was emitted on any of the 3 publisher
Fall clips by the original frozen classifier or legacy heuristic.

| Streaming categorical count | ADL 8 videos | Fall 3 videos |
|---|---:|---:|
| Decoded frames | 1,838 | 1,558 |
| Strict accepted single-person torso frames | 1,520 | 698 |
| All multi-pose frames (strictly abstained) | **51** | **860** |
| Short-lived unique-looking geometrical candidate | **3** | **108** |
| Multiple-pose frames lacking a fresh single anchor | 12 | **742** |
| Multi-pose frames with ambiguous near candidates | 28 | 0 |
| Multi-pose frames with no near candidate | 0 | 3 |
| Multi-pose frames with unusable geometry | 8 | 7 |
| More-than-two-pose unsupported | 0 | 0 |

The remainder of each multi-pose row reconciles exactly with strict
previous adapter rejection counts and 11-video source frame totals.
Single-pose accepted ADL frames include 49 with torso geometry too
small to anchor, so actual `single_anchor` count there was 1,471.

Individual Fall reference observations:

- Forward Fall walking: **1** unique-looking short-anchor candidate,
  389 stale/no anchor, 3 no match, 2 unusable geometry among 395
  strictly abstained multi-pose frames.
- Backward Fall: **107** unique-looking short-anchor candidates,
  353 stale/no anchor and 5 unusable among 465 multi-pose frames.
- Vertical Fall/fainting: no multi-pose; all **453/453** strict torso
  frames accepted; nevertheless no Fall candidate detected.

**Interpretation:** A conservative six-frame seed from *strict*
single-pose video samples finds at most **108 tentative continuity
hypotheses in 860** Fall multi-pose frames, and it cannot cover
**742** frames where the old anchor has expired or was unavailable.
This study does not yield verified identity tracking, recovered
PoseFrames, new fall classifications or improved sensitivity. These
numbers cannot justify overriding the production abstention policy.
We do not know the actual number of people visible in each clip,
and the subjects/onsets are not independently adjudicated.

The local report exists at:
`D:\VIIL-Sentinel\data\private\assist-temporal-association-20261027.json`.
Only public action activity labels and per-category integer counts
were saved. No individual trajectory, coordinates, subject IDs,
video image, model weight or frame-level pose scores were persisted.

## Concrete next developer requirements

Collect **new consented, independently annotated multi-person footage**
with known actor correspondences and crowded/occluded cases to measure
identity-switch errors and abstention, without optimizing against this
already exposed video subset. Independently label Fall onset times
and improve a separate research temporal classifier on new subject- and
camera-disjoint development data; preserve a fresh untouched test cohort.
Only after reliable identity/fall metrics plus false alerts/hour and
CPU/FPS/latency measurement may a human evaluate changing any live
multi-person gate or safety incident behavior.
